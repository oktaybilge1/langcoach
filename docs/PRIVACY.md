# Privacy Policy — LangCoach Browser Extension

**Effective date:** October 21, 2026

## Summary
LangCoach **does not collect, transmit, store on external servers, or share any personal data or browsing history**. All learning data stays 100% on your own device inside your browser's local storage (`chrome.storage.local`).

## Data Collection & Network Usage
- **No personal data collected:** LangCoach does not require an account, email address, or API key.
- **No network requests:** The extension never makes outbound network requests to any server, never calls Google's APIs itself, and includes no analytics, crash reporting, or telemetry.
- **Local-only storage:** Your language profile, weekly plan, captured vocabulary cards, review history, and settings are stored exclusively on your device via `chrome.storage.local`. You can export or delete this data at any time from the Settings tab.

## Permissions Explained
Each browser permission requested in `manifest.json` is used solely for local functionality:

- `storage`: Saves your learning profile, weekly study plan, flashcards, and progress locally on your device.
- `unlimitedStorage`: Prevents browser storage quota limits from truncating your flashcard deck and automatic safety backups as your vocabulary grows.
- `sidePanel`: Displays the LangCoach side panel (Today's tasks, Review flashcards, Weekly Plan, Progress, and Settings).
- `contextMenus`: Adds a "Save to LangCoach" right-click menu item on selected text on `https://labs.google/lll/*` pages.
- `alarms`: Schedules your optional daily study reminder at the local time you choose.
- `notifications`: Shows a local desktop notification when your optional daily study reminder fires.
- Host permission `https://labs.google/lll/*`: Runs only on Little Language Lessons pages to observe lesson responses generated by your own actions and display the on-page save toast.
